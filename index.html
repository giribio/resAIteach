Giri’s Collection of AI tools for research and teaching

Start by choosing Teaching or Research below, then pick a tool. Each tool comes with what it is for, the exact steps with a drawing of the screen at each step, a working demo you can try right here, a tip and a warning. Every example uses real undergraduate, MSc and research science content.

About the screens. They are drawings of each tool's workflow, made to show where things are and what to click. They are not captures of the live products, whose layouts and button names change often.
