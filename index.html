University science teaching · hands-on

Twelve tools, walked through step by step

For each tool you get what it is for, the exact steps with a drawing of the screen at each step, a working demo you can try right here, and a teaching tip. Every example uses real undergraduate and MSc science content: enzyme kinetics, Arrhenius, titration, projectile motion, protein structure.

About the screens. They are drawings of each tool's workflow, made to show where things are and what to click. They are not captures of the live products, whose layouts and button names change often. Highlighted outlines mark the control to use. Every “Try it here” panel is a working demo that runs inside this page, using example data.
